# Rubric: Authenticity

Grade ONE dimension: does the article read as lived, practised expertise rather than a summary of what other articles say?

PASS: Several sections contain specifics only a practitioner would include, such as a named decision, a real trade-off, a mistake and its fix, or a precise scene. These come from the author's interview or bundle and are attributed. Section rhythm varies.
FAIL: Swapping a few nouns would make it an article on a different topic. Every H2 has the same shape (context → generic tip → transition). Experience claims are vague ("in my experience, alignment matters").
UNKNOWN: You cannot tell. Do not default to PASS.

Output exactly:
VERDICT: PASS | FAIL | UNKNOWN
EVIDENCE: <quote the line that most drove the verdict>
FIX-STEP: <sai-cc-draft | sai-cc-sme-interview | none>
