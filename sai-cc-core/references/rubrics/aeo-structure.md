# Rubric: AEO Structure

Grade ONE dimension: could an answer engine lift a correct, self-contained answer from the definition, from each H2's opening, and from each FAQ?

Check:
1. A 40–60 word definition directly under the H1 that starts with the primary term.
2. Each H2's first 1–3 sentences answer the heading's implied question.
3. FAQ questions read like real queries, not section headings with a question mark added. Each answer is 40–80 words and stands alone.
4. Comparisons and processes are in tables or ordered lists.

PASS: all four hold (at most one minor miss on point 2).
FAIL: no answer-first definition; or two or more H2s open with throat-clearing; or FAQ answers depend on surrounding context.

Output exactly:
VERDICT: PASS | FAIL | UNKNOWN
EVIDENCE: <the section or FAQ that drove the verdict>
FIX-STEP: <sai-cc-seo-aeo | sai-cc-outline | none>
