# Rubric: Voice Match

Inputs: the author bundle's communication-style file, the identity files used (`identity_lens` in the frontmatter), and `07-final.md`.

Check:
1. Register: does it match the described voice (e.g. conversational-expert, direct, practical, no hype)?
2. Stance: are the article's positions consistent with the beliefs/principles file? Quote any contradictions.
3. Identity fidelity: does it avoid claiming expertise or status the bundle rules out (e.g. an aspirational identity presented as an established practice, or deep technical claims where the bundle says not to make them)?
4. Attributed experience: is every first-person anecdote traceable to `05-interview.md` or a bundle story?

Output exactly:
VERDICT: PASS | FAIL | UNKNOWN
EVIDENCE: <quote>
UNTRACEABLE-ANECDOTES: <list or none>
FIX-STEP: <sai-cc-draft | sai-cc-humanize | none>
